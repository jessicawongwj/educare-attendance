Attendance Monitoring

Privacy Notice: Educare College collects your name, Student ID, and attendance details for the purpose of recording class attendance. Your location is captured only at the moment of check-in and check-out to verify on-campus presence — it is not stored, tracked, or used for any other purpose. Attendance records may be disclosed to the Australian Skills Quality Authority (ASQA), NCVER, and relevant government agencies in accordance with the Data Provision Requirements 2012. All personal information is handled in compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). For privacy concerns, contact admin@educare.edu.au.

Declaration & Compliance
Attendance Policy
Educare College requires a minimum of 80% attendance for all enrolled courses.

Failure to meet this requirement may result in:
  • Course progress review
  • Intervention support meeting
  • Risk of course cancellation
If you are unable to attend, please notify your trainer or contact studentservices@educare.edu.au in advance.
ESOS & Visa Compliance (International Students)
As an international student, your attendance is monitored under the Education Services for Overseas Students (ESOS) Act 2000 and the National Code of Practice.

Your attendance is directly linked to your student visa conditions. Consistently low attendance may be reported to the Department of Home Affairs (DHA), which could affect your visa status.

For support or concerns: studentservices@educare.edu.au
