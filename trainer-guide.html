Logging In
  1. Open your browser and go to attendance.educare.edu.au.
  2. Click Sign in with Microsoft.
  3. Enter your Educare work email (e.g. name@educare.edu.au) and Microsoft password.
  4. The portal automatically loads your class based on your login email.
You stay logged in while you're using the portal. After a period of inactivity you'll be signed out — just sign in again.
If you see Access Denied after signing in, your email hasn't been linked to a trainer account yet. Contact your admin.
Always use your Educare work email (@educare.edu.au), not a personal address. Personal emails will be denied access.
Daily Attendance Tab

The Daily Attendance tab shows every student who has checked in via the QR code for the selected date. This is your real-time roll.

What you'll see

Checked In list

Students who scanned the QR code today, with their check-in time, check-out time (if scanned), course, and campus.

Not Checked In list

Your enrolled active students who haven't scanned yet today. Use this to quickly spot who's missing and manually mark them.

Changing the date

Use the date picker at the top of the tab to view any past date's roll. The portal defaults to today (Brisbane time).

Searching & filtering

  • Search box — type a name or Student ID to filter instantly
  • Status filter — show only Present, Absent, or All
The daily roll only shows QR check-ins. Students who attended but didn't scan must be manually marked — see Marking Attendance.
A red Suspicious badge may appear next to a Present record — hover it to see why (a check-in time in the future, an unusual hour, or GPS that doesn't match any campus). It's a flag to double-check, not an automatic rejection; the record still counts as present.
My Class Tab

The My Class tab shows all students enrolled in your class, organised by course. It is automatically filtered to your trainer name.

Course filter pills

If you teach more than one course, coloured pills appear at the top — one per course (showing the course code and short name). Click a pill to view only that course's students. Click All Courses to see everyone.

A superseded training-package code and its current replacement (e.g. CHC50121 → CHC50125) share one pill rather than splitting into two — students on either code for the same real course show up together.

Sub-tabs

Sub-tabWho appears here
Not Checked In Active students who haven't scanned today, including students who have attended before but not yet today
Tentative Newly enrolled students who have never attended. They move to Not Checked In automatically once they scan for the first time.

Actions from My Class

  • Click a student's name to open their full attendance history
  • Click Mark next to a student to quickly record their attendance for today
  • Use Export CSV to download your full class list
My Class is the best place to take roll if students haven't scanned — you can mark everyone present or absent quickly without switching views.
Marking Attendance Manually

Manual marking is used when a student attended but forgot to scan, or when you need to correct an error.

Mark a student present (single day)

  1. Go to My Class or Daily Attendance and find the student in the Not Checked In list.
  2. Click Mark next to their name.
  3. Select Present (or On Placement — see below), optionally adjust the check-in time, add a note if needed.
  4. Click Save. The student moves to the Checked In list immediately.

Mark a student absent (remove an incorrect check-in)

  1. Find the student in the Checked In list.
  2. Click their name or the Mark Absent button.
  3. Confirm, then click Save. The record is removed.

Mark attendance for multiple past dates

  1. Click the student's name anywhere in the portal to open their history modal.
  2. Click Mark Attendance inside the modal.
  3. Choose Mark Absent, Mark Present, or On Placement, select one or more dates, add a note.
  4. Click Save.

On Placement

On Placement is a proper status option, not just a note — it appears in the Mark Attendance modal and the inline + Attendance form's Status dropdown. It writes a normal Present record (counts toward attendance rate exactly the same as a regular Present day) but shows a distinct blue Placement badge everywhere attendance is displayed — Daily Attendance, Student History, and the history CSV export — so it's easy to tell placement days apart from a scanned check-in or manual correction at a glance.

Add attendance directly from My Class (+ Attendance button)

Each student row in My Class has a green + Attendance button below the Remove button. This opens an inline form without leaving the page:

  • Date — single date (defaults to today)
  • To (range, optional) — fill a second date to mark a whole range at once; weekends are automatically skipped
  • Status — Present, On Placement, or Absent
  • Note — free text, or click a quick-note chip: Placement, Medical Certificate, Personal, Absent — No Contact, Late Arrival

Click Save to write directly to the database. A confirmation toast appears and the form closes. Click Cancel to dismiss without saving.

For a placement block, use the On Placement status with a date range — one save marks the whole week and shows the badge on every day, rather than typing "Placement" as a free-text note on a regular Present.
Manual present records include a note showing who marked them and when, so the audit trail is always clear.
Marking Absent permanently removes the attendance record for that date. This cannot be undone from the portal — contact your admin if you need to restore a record.
Rate Check Tab

The Rate Check tab shows each student's attendance percentage, calculated from their course commencement date using the academic calendar (weekends, public holidays, and school breaks excluded).

The calculation currently assumes 2 sessions per week for every course, rather than each course's actual scheduled days. If your class runs on a different number of days per week, treat the displayed rate as indicative rather than exact for now — this is a known limitation being reviewed.

Columns explained

ColumnMeaning
RateOverall attendance % from commencement date to today
MonthlyAttendance % for the current calendar month only
Last Check-InMost recent date the student scanned or was marked present
CommencedCourse start date — rate is calculated from here
Expected EndCourse end date from their enrollment record

At-Risk students

Students with a rate below 80% are flagged At Risk. These students should be contacted and supported to improve attendance.

Filtering

  • Status filter — show All, At Risk only, or Good Standing only
  • Search box — search by name or student ID

Print Class List

Click Print Class List at the top of the Rate Check tab to generate a printable sign-in sheet grouped by course, including columns for Student ID, Campus, Commenced date, and a Signature field. Use this for paper roll-calls or compliance audits.

Click any student's name in Rate Check to open their full attendance history and see exactly which days they attended.
Student Attendance History

Click any student's name from any tab to open their history modal.

What the history modal shows

  • Course name, commencement date, and expected end date
  • Overall attendance rate
  • All check-in dates shown as date pills — scroll to see the full history
  • Most recent dates appear at the top

Actions from the history modal

  • Mark Attendance — add or remove records for specific dates
  • Export CSV — download this student's full attendance history as a spreadsheet
  • Add Note — attach a note to the student's record (visible to admins and trainers)
The history shows records from the student's commencement date with no time limit — all historical check-ins are visible.
Holidays Tab

The Holidays tab is a read-only reference showing every date students aren't expected to attend — Queensland public holidays and Educare's scheduled school breaks.

Public Holidays

Lists every Queensland public holiday for the year, split into Upcoming and Past. The next holiday is also highlighted at the top of the tab. These dates are excluded automatically from every attendance rate calculation — you don't need to do anything for them.

School Breaks

Lists the scheduled term-break periods from the Educare Academic Calendar (Easter, mid-year, spring, and year-end breaks). A break that's currently in progress is marked NOW. Students aren't expected during these periods and the days are excluded from attendance rate calculations, the same as public holidays.

This tab is informational only — there's nothing to mark or edit here. If a date looks wrong, contact your admin.

Emailing Today's Roll

You can email yourself a copy of today's attendance roll at any time.

  1. Go to the Daily Attendance tab.
  2. Make sure the date and trainer filter are set to your class.
  3. Click the Email Roll button (envelope icon in the toolbar).
  4. The roll is sent to your Educare email address.
The email arrives from the Educare shared mailbox within a minute. Check your spam folder if it doesn't appear.
Tips & Common Questions

A student says they scanned but isn't showing up

Ask them to check that they entered their Student ID exactly as it appears in their enrolment letter (no spaces, correct capitalisation). The system only records check-ins for IDs that exist in the roster. If the ID is correct, manually mark them present and notify your admin so the ID can be verified.

The portal is showing the wrong class

The portal matches your class by your login email. If you're seeing another trainer's class, contact your admin — your email may be mapped to the wrong trainer account.

A student is enrolled but not appearing in My Class

The student may have a different campus assigned in their enrollment record. Contact your admin to verify their campus and trainer assignment.

I can see students from another trainer

If you have admin access you'll see all trainers. Use the trainer filter dropdown to narrow to your class, or ask your admin to remove your admin access if it's not needed.

The attendance rate looks wrong for a student

The rate is calculated from the student's commencement date using the academic calendar. If their commenced date is wrong in the system, the rate will be incorrect — contact your admin to fix the enrollment record.
